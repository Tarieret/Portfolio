About

Hi! I'm Tariere Timitimi, and I am a data professional with a background in data science and applied statistics. I have a Master's in Data Science from St. John's University and a BA in Political Science from Adelphi University, and I have four years of experience, including two in data analytics, and BI/dashboard development. To me, good data work starts with the right question and ends with something people can act on.

I am passionate about continuously learning, currently I hold the AWS Certified Machine Learning Engineer – Associate certification, along with Databricks and NVIDIA credentials, amongst other certifications, and I write about my projects on Portfolio Notes, my Substack.

Outside of work, I'm a 2026 Women in Data Science (WiDS) Ambassador, a role where I support their mission to empower women in Data Science and AI.
